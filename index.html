01 Research focus

From material structure
to mechanical behaviour.

Soft materials are shaped by their internal architecture and loading history. My research asks how to capture that complexity in models that can be formulated, computed and compared with evidence.

The research overview
01

Constitutive modelling

Mathematical descriptions of nonlinear elasticity and time-dependent behaviour in soft tissues and biomimetic materials.

 02

Microstructure & mechanics

Representative volume elements that connect fibre architecture with the response of the material as a whole.

 03

Scientific computation

Finite element methods, numerical implementation and parameter identification for questions in biomechanics.

An approach to research

03 The researcher

Grounded in mechanics.

I am a Ph.D. candidate and Research Assistant at Ege University, and a Guest Researcher at the Institute of Biomechanics, TU Graz. My work brings together soft tissue mechanics, material modelling and scientific programming.

Alongside research, I contribute to teaching in mechanics and to laboratory work in material characterisation.
