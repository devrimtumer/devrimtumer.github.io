Research & perspective

Between theory
and computation.

I am a Ph.D. candidate and Research Assistant in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Ege University, and a Guest Researcher at the Institute of Biomechanics, Graz University of Technology.

My research centres on computational biomechanics and the constitutive modelling of soft biological and biomimetic materials. I work across mathematical formulation, numerical implementation and comparison with experimental data, with interests in hyperelasticity, viscoelasticity, parameter identification and multiscale modelling.

My background in mechanical engineering includes research on shoulder biomechanics, implant materials and thermal analysis. These applications share a common thread: using mechanics and computation to examine a physical system in a precise, testable way.
